# Rail and ports

> *Rail · cargo train* / *Sea · freighter*

Rail and ports are how your city moves freight. Lundby has four
freight modes: rail, sea, road, and air. The **RAIL & PORTS** section
of the build menu is where most of them live.

## Cargo rail

*Cargo rail terminal* / *Cargo rail*

Cargo rail is the high-volume land freight mode. It runs on surface
railway to terminals at the edge of the city.

### How to set up a cargo rail terminal
1. Build a **Cargo rail terminal** on a flat site with railway access.
2. *Each station needs its own platform along the railway.*
3. *Connect an operating rail depot with straight, level railway along
> its full side.*
4. Build a **Rail depot** beside the terminal.
5. Build surface railway to the **map edge** for regional freight
> service.
6. *Build a road to the map boundary to establish the regional yard.*
7. Purchase a **cargo train** at the terminal.
8. *Purchase a cargo train at this terminal first.*

> *Locate regional yard* — the link in the management panel.

### Cargo rail reports
- *Cargo locomotives* / *Cargo wagons* — the fleet.
- *Cargo and freight paid; stock becomes usable on arrival.* — the
> settlement timing.
- *Cargo paid / carrier fee* — the invoice.
- *Freight / shopping cargo* — the mix.
- *Rail · cargo train* — the legend label.
- *RAIL FREIGHT* — the section header.
- *Cargo underframes* / *Freight bogies* / *Freight containers* /
> *Cargo glazing* — the parts that make a freight train.

## Trade depot

The trade depot is where imports and exports meet the region.

> *Found a region to exchange <commodity> between your cities.*
> *Import to target* / *Export from source* — the trade actions.
> *Purchase from your neighbors* — the suggestion.

### How to set up a trade depot
1. Build a **Trade depot** beside a regional road.
2. *Build a road to the map boundary to establish the regional yard.*
3. Open the **Region & trade** panel and configure your trade routes.

> *Automatic regional trading* — the autopilot toggle.
> *Offer local control capacity* / *Offer this city's surplus* —
> the menu.
> *Purchase agreement active.* — the confirmation.
> *Purchase agreement updated. New service is billed on actual city
> days.* — the timing note.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **Cargo rail is a volume play.** Small loads go by road; bulk cargo
  goes by rail.
- **Ports need open water.** *Restore an open water edge beside this
> quay.*
- **Ferries and freighters share the water.** Make sure the channel
  is clear.
- **The trade depot is the gate.** Without a regional road connection,
> the trade depot is just a building.
- **Recalls are slow.** A recalled truck or carrier has to physically
> return before the refund posts.
