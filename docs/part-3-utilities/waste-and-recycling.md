# Waste and recycling

> *From waste to resources.*

Every household and every business produces waste. The waste system is
what keeps the city clean and turns what would be rubbish back into raw
materials.

## The waste cycle

1. **Generation** — households and businesses produce waste.
2. **Collection** — collection trucks pick it up.
3. **Transport** — to a landfill, a recycling center, or a recovery
   plant.
4. **Disposal or recovery** — buried, recycled, or turned back into raw
   materials.

> *A collection truck record is damaged.* — the kind of internal error
> that surfaces if a save gets corrupted.

## The buildings

### Collection truck garage
*Collection truck garage*

- Houses the trucks that pick up waste.
- *Two additional collection trucks. Processing and storage stay
> unchanged.* — the annex that adds more trucks without expanding
> the processing plant.

### Landfill
*Sanitary landfill*

- The basic disposal site. *Build landfill* opens the tool.
- *Landfill storage* — the report line that shows how full it is.

### Recycling center
*Build recycling*

- *Reduce household waste by 35%.* — a research-driven boost.
- *Recycling hall* — the high-tier annex.

### Ground recovery plant
*Ground recovery plant*

- Cleans contaminated soil.
- *Recovers collected waste* — recovers materials from the waste
  stream.

### Optical sorting hall
*Optical sorting hall*

- High-capacity, automated recycling.
- *From waste to resources* — the flavor line.

## Collection routes

Collection trucks follow a route that the city optimizes. As long as
they have a road, they'll find bins.

- *Locate collection truck #X* — finds a specific truck on the map.
- *Both delivery trucks are on the road; wait for a return* — a status
  you'll see when the fleet is busy.
- *Collection truck* — the small icon on the map.

## The waste map

The waste data layer shows the waste network.

- **Red** — waste is uncollected.
- **Green** — waste is being collected.
- *Waste awaiting pickup* — the icon on a building.

> *Keep collection moving* — the message when collection is failing.

## Waste reports

- *Waste & recycling* — the management menu section.
- *Recycled to date* — the all-time counter.
- *Exported / disposed* — what happened to the rest.
- *Regional disposal to date* — across the whole region.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **Don't let the landfill overflow.** An over-full landfill stops
  accepting waste, and the city grinds to a halt.
- **Recycling is cheaper than landfill** in the long run, but the
  upfront cost is higher.
- **A collection truck needs a road.** Buildings on a disconnected
  block will see their waste pile up.
- **Recovery is the endgame.** A ground recovery plant turns waste
  back into raw materials, which closes the loop with industry.
