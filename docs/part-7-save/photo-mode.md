# Photo mode

> *A moment worth keeping.*

Photo mode is a stripped-down camera UI for framing and capturing
your city. It hides the build UI, lets you change the time of day,
and saves the result as a PNG.

## Where to find it

- **City → Photo mode**
- Or click the camera button in the toolbar.

> *Photo mode* / *Photos mode* — the link.
> *Photo mode* — the section.

## Entering photo mode

1. Open the **Photo mode** panel.
2. The HUD hides, the camera UI shows.
3. Frame your shot.

> *A moment worth keeping.* — the flavor line.

## What you can do

### Frame the shot
- **Pan** with left-drag or **W / A / S / D**.
- **Orbit** with right-drag or **Q / E**.
- **Zoom** with the mouse wheel.
- **Reset** with the reset button.
- **Rotate** with the rotate buttons.

### Change the time of day
- Use the time-of-day slider to set the time manually.
- Override the day-night cycle.
- Useful for golden hour, blue hour, and night shots.

> *Time of day* / *Change time of day* — the control.

### Hide the HUD
- The build UI, the demand panel, and the data layers are hidden.
- The simulation keeps running in the background.

### Record a video
- *Record 10 seconds* — the action.
- The game records a 10-second clip of the camera.

> *Recording* / *Recorded* / *JUST NOW* — the status.

### Save the photograph
- *Save photograph* — the action.
- *Download PNG* — the action.
- *Exported photograph of <city name>* — the file name.

> *A moment in <name>* — the title for the photograph.
> *A moment worth keeping.* — the flavor line.

## Exporting the photograph

> *Download PNG* / *Download* / *Download requested. Your preview
> stays available here.* — the download actions.
> *Save photograph* — the save action.
> *The image is ready to download.* — the confirmation.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **Time of day is the biggest lever.** A 6 pm shot is very
  different from a noon shot.
- **Hide the HUD for a clean shot.** Most of the chrome is
  unnecessary for a photograph.
- **The simulation is still running.** Cars, people, and weather
  continue. A pause first might be useful.
- **Photo mode is a city feature.** Each city has its own photo
> mode session.
