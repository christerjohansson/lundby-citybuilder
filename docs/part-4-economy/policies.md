# Policies

> *A promise kept.*

Policies are the citywide levers that change how the simulation runs.
They're set in the management menu and applied to the whole city.

## The policy categories

### Tax policy
*Tax policy*

- The most direct lever.
- *Tax rates* — the sliders.
- *Use income-specific residential tax rates* — the toggle.
- *Set separate residential rates for each income group.* — the
> instruction.
- *The population-weighted residential rate is* — the summary.
- *Tax controls* — the menu link.

See [Treasury and budget](treasury-and-budget.md) for the full
walkthrough.

### Clean industry standard
*Clean industry standard*

- *Cut industrial pollution 45 %; industry tax receipts fall 15 %.*
- The trade-off: less pollution, less revenue.

### Sustainability pressure
*Sustainability pressure*

- *Move heavy industry away from homes and add parks or clean
> industry standards.* — the advice.
- A multi-effect policy that rewards clean industry and penalises
> heavy industry.

### Energy efficiency
*Energy efficiency*

- *Reduce electricity demand by 25 %.* — the milestone effect.
- *Research reduces citywide power consumption by 20 %.* — the
> research effect.

### Water conservation
*Water conservation*

- *Reduce water consumption by 30 %.* — the milestone effect.
- Pairs with the Utilities department.

### Education funding
*Education funding*

- *Education funding must be at least 40 %.* — the floor.
- *Raise education funding to at least 40 %.* — the warning.
- *Citywide education improvement of 15 points.* — the milestone
> effect.

### Transport funding
*Transport funding*

- *Increase transport funding to staff a gate* — the operational
> nudge.
- *Restore transport funding* — the warning.
- *Restore transport funding to run service* — the more specific
> warning.
- *Service funding* — the budget line.

### Public safety
*Public safety*

- *Reduce disaster damage and speed up fire response.* — the research
> effect.
- *Police protection across the city.* — the Safety department
> promise.

### Education initiative
*Education initiative*

- A second education lever.
- *Citywide education improvement of 15 points.* — the milestone
> effect.
- *Reduce household waste by 35 %.* — the cross-cutting effect.

### Emergency preparedness
*Emergency preparedness*

- *Reduce disaster damage and speed up fire response.* — the effect.
- *Ready for the unexpected.* — the flavor line.

## How to set a policy

1. Open the **City policies** panel from the management menu.
2. Find the policy in the list.
3. Toggle or set the value.
4. The effect applies citywide on the next day.

> *City policies* / *Policy for* — the menu items.
> *Upgrades paused by the mayor* — the warning when a policy pauses
> construction.

## The policy report

> *Housing & education policy review* — the report header.
> *Review housing & education policy* — the menu link.

The report shows the active policies and their measured effects.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **Policies are slow.** A policy change takes days to show up in the
  numbers.
- **Tax rates drive behavior.** A high commercial tax drives
  businesses out; a high residential tax drives residents out.
- **Education and transport are sticky.** Once you set them above
  40 %, the residents notice and stay.
- **Sustainability is a long game.** The pressure policy costs you
  now to save you later.
