# City Hall and council

> *A council for your city.*

City Hall is where the city is governed. It hosts the mayor's office, the
council chambers, and the six department annexes that unlock the city's
deeper tools.

## City Hall

The first City Hall you build is **Lundby City Hall**. Place it on a
road-accessible site with power and water.

> *A city hall for a city with ambition.*

### The building
- **LUNDBY CITY HALL** — the standard City Hall.
- *City Hall & departments* — the management menu section.

### How to build it
1. Open the **City Hall** category in the build menu.
2. Pick **Lundby City Hall** (or your unlocked variant).
3. *Needs working roads, power and water.*
4. Place it on a road-accessible site.

> *Build City Hall ·* — the prompt when the city is missing City Hall.
> *Finish building City Hall first.* — the prerequisite warning.
> *Build an operating City Hall first.* — another variant.

## The council

> *CITY COUNCIL* — the section in the management menu.

The council has **six department slots**. Each slot is earned by
reaching a population milestone. Each slot hosts one department annex
on the side of City Hall.

> *Council expanded. Choose and build a department annex.*
> *All six department slots are earned.* — when you've unlocked all
> six.
> *All six department slots earned. Population loss does not revoke
> earned slots.* — the reassurance.
> *Population toward next council slot* — the progress meter.
> *Population and household report* — the population report.
> *Your council has room to grow* — the empty-slot flavor line.

### The six departments

#### Finance
*Finance department*

- Unlocks the deeper budget tools.
- *An operating Finance department in the connected region is
> required.* — for some regional actions.

#### Education
*Education department*

- Unlocks education policy and the research projects.
- *Education funding must be at least 40 %.* — the policy floor.
- *Raise education funding to at least 40 %.* — the warning.
- *Education initiative* — the policy tool.

#### Safety
*Safety department*

- Improves emergency response.
- *Emergency preparedness* — the policy tool.
- *Reduce disaster damage and speed up fire response.* — the research
> effect.

#### Transport
*Transport department*

- Improves transit effectiveness.
- *Double transit effectiveness and improve happiness.* — the effect.
- *Increase transport funding to staff a gate* — the operational
> nudge.

#### Tourism
*Tourism department*

- Unlocks the events and tourism tools.
- *Open events & tourism* — the menu link.

#### Utilities
*Utilities department*

- Improves the utility networks.
- *Energy efficiency* — the policy tool.
- *Water conservation* — the policy tool.

## How to add a department

1. Reach the population milestone for the next slot.
2. Open the **City Hall & departments** panel.
3. Pick a department.
4. *Attach the department to the highlighted City Hall · R to
> rotate.*
5. *Build the department annex.*
6. *Earn another council department slot before adding this annex.* —
> the prerequisite warning.

> *Claim department slot · No fee* — the no-cost option when sandbox
> is on.
> *Council progression* — the report.

## Council progression

The council grows as your city grows. The progress bar shows how many
residents you need for the next slot.

> *A city finding its rhythm* / *A city of every generation* /
> *A city that takes care.* — the flavor lines for the early
> milestones.

## Sharing with the region

A city with a built department can share it with connected
municipalities.

> *Connect this city to a regional road to share this department.*
> *Connect City Hall to a regional road.* — the prerequisite.
> *Reconnect this city's regional road.* — the warning.

When shared, the department's services are available to the whole
region.

> *No department built in the region yet.* — the message when no
> connected city has the department.
> *Operating · serving the region* — the status.
> *Serving the region* — the short status.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **Build City Hall early.** Many later buildings need it.
- **Don't rush the population.** Departments cost money to run; build
  them when you can afford them.
- **Share wisely.** A shared department supports the whole region but
  adds running cost to your city.
- **Reconnect when you rebuild.** A broken regional road cuts off the
  shared services.
