# Bonds and development

> *A new beginning.*

Bonds are how the city borrows money in the short term. **Development
bonds** are a special kind: they fund large projects that the regular
budget can't cover.

## The two kinds of bonds

### Regular bonds
*Issue $25,000 bond*

- The basic borrowing tool.
- *The city can hold at most three active bonds.*
- *Credit is unavailable while treasury is below −$50,000.* — the
> credit limit.
- *Bond paid off* — when a bond matures.
- *Bond repayments* — the monthly line item.

### Development bonds
*Development bonds*

- Used for large projects: regional infrastructure, great works, and
> new city charters.
- *Development bond issued* — the credit line.
- *Development in progress* — the project status.
- *DEVELOPMENT WAITING* / *DEVELOPMENT IN PROGRESS* — the project
> states.

## How to issue a regular bond

1. Open the **Treasury** panel.
2. Click **Issue bond**.
3. Pick a size ($25,000 is the standard).
4. Pick a term and an interest rate.
5. Confirm.

> *Not enough uncommitted funds for this fuel invoice.* — the warning
> if the treasury is too tight.

## How development bonds work

A development bond is tied to a specific project.

1. Pick a project — a great work, a regional infrastructure project,
   or a new city charter.
2. The project shows its cost and its funding source.
3. *SHARED REGIONAL PROJECT* — when the project is shared across the
   region.
4. *Funded ·* — when the bond is in place.
5. *Project cleared. Any undelivered supplies are returning.* — when
> the project is complete.

> *An unfunded regional project cannot be under construction.* — the
> warning.

## Regional project funding

When a project is shared across the region, the funding is shared
too.

> *Regional project funding* — the report.
> *Shared regional project* — the section header.
> *Your regional great work is now serving connected cities.* — the
> confirmation when the project is online.
> *View regional great works* — the menu link.

## The credit line

A separate concept: the city's **credit** is the headroom it has for
new bonds.

> *Credit is unavailable while treasury is below −$50,000.*
> *Not enough funds for one day of the required control capacity.* —
> the related message.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **Three bonds is the cap.** Plan your borrowing.
- **A bond's interest is the price.** A 3 % bond on $25 000 costs
  $750 over a year.
- **Development bonds are for big projects.** A regular bond is
  cheaper for short-term needs.
- **The cap resets on repayment.** When a bond matures, the slot
  opens up.
