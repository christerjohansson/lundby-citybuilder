# Milestones and grants

> *Time to write a new chapter.*

Milestones are the major goals the city hits as it grows. Each one
unlocks a grant — a one-time payment from the regional council — and
sometimes a new tool or policy.

## The milestones

The major milestones you'll see are:

| Milestone | Grant | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Welcome your first residents** | A starter grant | The first building tools |
| **Your first school** | *Your first school starts a lifetime of possibility.* | Education policies |
| **Your first community takes root** | A community grant | Public-safety policies |
| **Reach 5,000 residents** | *Citywide education improvement of 15 points.* | Council slots |
| **Reach 8,000 residents** | *Citywide health improvement of 12 points.* | More council slots |
| **Reach 20,000 residents** | A major grant | The full council |
| **Reach 80 % education with 2,500 residents** | An education grant | The Education department |
| **Keep pollution below 10 with 5,000 residents** | An environment grant | The Environment department |
| **Maintain 90 % happiness with 1,000 residents** | A happiness grant | The Tourism department |
| **Reduce electricity demand by 25 %** | An efficiency grant | The Utilities department |
| **Reduce water consumption by 30 %** | A water grant | The Utilities department |
| **Reduce household waste by 35 %** | A waste grant | The Waste department |
| **Place 100 improvements** | A heritage grant | A district upgrade |
| **Complete a city challenge** | A challenge grant | The challenge complete state |
| **Complete development to unlock** | A development grant | A new development |

> *City achievements* — the management menu section.
> *Milestone* / *Milestones* — the report.

## How milestones work

A milestone is a goal with a measurable threshold: a population, a
happiness, a pollution level, a research effect. When you cross the
threshold, the grant is paid automatically.

> *Milestone grant · <milestone>* — the credit line in the treasury.
> *Milestone grant* — the budget line.

## Grants vs. the budget

Grants are **windfall income** — they appear as a one-time credit in
the treasury, with a memo line that names the milestone. They don't
fund the budget directly; they just add to the cash balance.

> *Earned tourism receipts* / *Earned airport passenger fees* / *Earned
> tourism receipts* — these are earned revenue, not grants.

## The city charter

A new city in the region needs a **charter**.

> *A new city charter costs $20,000 from this city's treasury.*
> *New city charter* — the action.
> *Current access follows this city's established charter.* — the
> status of an existing charter.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **Milestones are automatic.** You don't have to claim them; the
  treasury is credited when you cross the threshold.
- **Grants are one-time.** Once paid, they don't repeat.
- **The threshold matters.** *Keep pollution below 10 with 5,000
  residents* — both numbers are part of the goal.
- **Some milestones are challenges in disguise.** *Recover from a
  disaster with 1,000 residents* is the Recovery challenge's goal.
