# Choosing a start

> *A place to begin.*

Lundby gives you five ways to begin. Two are open-ended; three are timed
challenges. The right one depends on what you want from the game today.

## The five scenarios

### 1. Your own horizon
- **Type:** open-ended.
- **Flavor:** *Start from untouched land. Build at your own pace.*
- **For you if:** you want a sandbox with no clock.
- **Terrain:** any of the four terrain types. See [The terrain types](the-terrain-types.md).

### 2. Lundby Bay
- **Type:** open-ended, but you start in a living city.
- **Flavor:** *Take the reins of an established coastal city.*
- **For you if:** you want to skip the early game and dive into a city that
  already has zoning, services, and demand rolling.

### 3. Growth challenge
- **Goal:** reach 8 000 residents and 70 % happiness within 10 years.
- **For you if:** you want a soft timed goal that still leaves room to plan.

### 4. Environment challenge
- **Goal:** reach 5 000 residents, 80 % happiness, and pollution below 15
  within 8 years.
- **For you if:** you want to prove you can grow without choking the city.

### 5. Recovery challenge
- **Goal:** rebuild a city struck by a powerful earthquake. Recover from
  the disaster with 1 000 residents.
- **For you if:** you want a low-population start with a long recovery arc.

## After a challenge

When a challenge completes, the deadline passes and you can keep playing the
same city for as long as you like. There's no penalty for continuing — the
timer just stops mattering.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- Story scenarios use their original Standard map. You don't pick a terrain
  for them.
- *Lundby Bay* starts with demand already running. Don't be surprised when
  buildings start appearing before you zone anything.
- *Recovery* opens with damaged buildings and reduced services. Get a fire
  station and a clinic online early.
