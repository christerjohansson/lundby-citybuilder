# The title screen

> *A complete game in one file.*

The title screen is your front door. It shows the build number, a few
flavor lines, and the entry points for new and returning Mayors.

## What you see

- **Header:** *Lundby · A city, shaped by you.*
- **Sub-line:** *A destination, an economy, a home.*
- **Scenario cards:** five ways to begin. See [Choosing a start](choosing-a-start.md).
- **Footer / ambient:** a generative piano-like soundscape plays in the
  background (toggle in *Settings*).

## The two buttons

- **New city** — opens the start dialog. Pick a terrain, a difficulty, and
  a scenario.
- **Your cities** — opens your saved cities list. The game autosaves
  continuously and keeps the last three manual slots; your previous cities
  will appear here without needing a manual export.

## What the start dialog asks you

In order:

1. **Scenario** — the five cards.
2. **Map size** — small, medium, large, and open-ended.
3. **Terrain** — coastal bay, river valley, highlands, or islands.
4. **Difficulty** — *Relaxed*, *Standard*, or *Challenging*.

Story scenarios (the three challenges) use their original Standard map and
ignore difficulty.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- The title screen remembers the last origin you served it from. If you
  move to a different port, your saved cities will not appear — saves are
  per-browser, per-port.
- The first time you load the game, prepare a moment for the offline files
  to be cached. You'll see a "Checking this build's offline files…" message
  while it warms up.
- If a card is greyed out, that scenario is locked behind a milestone or a
  sandbox permission.
