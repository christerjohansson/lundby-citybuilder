# Tutorial walkthrough

> *Welcome your first residents.*

The tutorial walks you through your first city. It's a guided version of
*Your own horizon* on a small map. Follow the prompts, build what they ask,
and the city will start to come to life.

## Step 1 — Build your first road

> *A good job is worth keeping.*

1. Open the **Roads** tool from the build menu.
2. Pick the **two-lane neighborhood road** (the cheapest one).
3. Drag from the edge of the map inward. A short stub is enough to start.
4. The build menu will tell you the cost before you commit.

> **Tip:** roads carry power and water beneath them by default. You only
> need separate power lines and water pipes when you want utilities away
> from a road.

## Step 2 — Place a power source and a water source

> *From the ground to the tap.*

1. Open the **Power** tool and place a **coal power station** beside the
   road. (In sandbox or a relaxed difficulty, you can use a **wind turbine**
   or a **solar farm** instead.)
2. Open the **Water** tool and place a **water tower** beside the road.
3. The power pole that appears at the road's end carries electricity; the
   water tower pumps drinking water into the same network.

> **Tip:** roads automatically carry power and water between connected
> buildings. You only need power lines and water pipes once you want
> utilities to leave the road.

![alt: A coal power station and a water tower placed beside the new road. The road's edge has small utility icons (a lightning bolt and a water drop) indicating the network is connected. The demand panel on the right shows green bars for residential, commercial, and industrial demand.](../../assets/images/welcome/tutorial-power-water.png "Tutorial step 2 — power and water online")

## Step 3 — Zone some residential and a touch of commercial

1. Open the **Zoning** tool.
2. Pick the **residential** brush and paint a couple of blocks along the
   road.
3. Switch to the **commercial** brush and paint a small commercial strip
   at the end of the road.
4. Watch the demand panel — if the bars are green, growth is coming.

> **Tip:** you don't need industrial for a small city, but you'll want some
> soon to give residents jobs and give businesses stock to sell.

![alt: A small two-block neighborhood: residential zoning along one side of the road, commercial zoning at the end. Three small residential buildings are already growing. The demand panel is in the corner.](../../assets/images/welcome/tutorial-zone.png "Tutorial step 3 — first zoning")

## Step 4 — Connect to the region

> *The first connection.*

1. Look for the dashed road on the edge of the map — that's your **regional
   connection** preview.
2. Build a road from your city to meet it.
3. As soon as a road touches the regional boundary, your city is
   connected. Imports, exports, and the regional services panel unlock.

> **Tip:** until you connect, you can only buy and sell within your own
> city. The connection is what turns a settlement into a region.

## Step 5 — Build City Hall

> *A council for your city.*

1. Open the **City Hall** tool and place **Lundby City Hall** on a road-
   accessible site.
2. Open the management menu and look at the **CITY COUNCIL** section.
3. As you reach population milestones, you'll earn council department
   slots. Build departments as annexes on City Hall to unlock Finance,
   Education, Safety, Transport, Tourism, and Utilities.

## Step 6 — Save

> *Your city, to keep.*

- The game autosaves continuously. You don't need to do anything.
- For a permanent backup, open the management menu and choose **Export
  city**. You'll get a `.lundby` file you can keep anywhere.
