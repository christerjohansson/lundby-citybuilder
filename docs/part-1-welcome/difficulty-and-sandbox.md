# Difficulty and sandbox

> *Make yourself at home.*

Difficulty is the budget pressure on your city. Sandbox is a permission that
unlocks the parts of the game that the council would normally gate.

## Difficulty

There are three levels:

| Level | Flavor | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Relaxed** | *Relaxed · lower running costs* | Slower drain on your treasury. Good for learning. |
| **Standard** | *Standard · balanced economy* | The default. The game expects you to balance the books. |
| **Challenging** | *Challenging · higher running costs* | Every line item costs a little more. Mistakes compound. |

Story scenarios (the three challenges) use their original Standard map and
ignore difficulty. You don't pick a difficulty for them.

## Sandbox

Sandbox mode unlocks the city's deeper tools without the council gates. In
sandbox:

- **All buildings unlocked.** The city simulation still runs, but you can
  build anything from the start.
- **Unlimited construction funds** — every tool is available, regardless
  of treasury.
- **Sandbox council** — the six council department slots are available
  immediately, instead of being earned.
- **Sandbox permits are available without an office.** You can build
  taller towers and bigger annexes without the usual prerequisites.

Sandbox permission is granted through the management menu, and persists
across your saved cities.

## When to use sandbox

- **Learning the game.** The simulation is fully on, but you don't have to
  worry about going broke while you experiment.
- **Photo mode runs.** When you just want to build a pretty scene.
- **Stress-testing.** When you want to see how a fully-built city copes
  with a disaster.

## When not to use sandbox

- If you want the milestones and grants to feel meaningful. Sandbox removes
  the city council progression that unlocks them.
- If you want bonds and development pressure. Sandbox's unlimited
  construction funds make bonds irrelevant.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- Sandbox is per-city, not per-browser. You can have one city in sandbox
  and another in Standard side by side.
- The "All buildings unlocked" note is the give-away — if a tool isn't
  grayed out, you're in sandbox.
- Sandbox permission is independent of the timed challenges. You can play
  a challenge in sandbox to learn its goal.
