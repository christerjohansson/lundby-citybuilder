# Visitors and tourism

> *Give people somewhere to go, a way to get there, and a place to spend the night.*

Visitors are the lifeblood of a tourism city. They arrive by plane,
train, ferry, and bus, stay in hotels, visit attractions, and leave
spending behind. The **Tourism** panel is your window into it.

## The tourism loop

1. **Arrival** — visitors come from the region or from other cities.
2. **Stay** — they book a hotel or a day-trip.
3. **Visit** — they go to attractions, events, and festivals.
4. **Spend** — they shop, eat, and book tickets.
5. **Departure** — they go home.

> *Ways into the city* — the arrival modes.

### How to add a hotel
1. Open the **Tourism** category in the build menu.
2. Pick a hotel type.
3. Place it near a transit node — *Ways into the city.*
4. The hotel will accept visitors automatically.

> *Hotel concession receipts* — the revenue line.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **Visitors need ways in.** *Ways into the city.* A city without an
  airport, a train station, or a ferry terminal is a city without
  visitors.
- **Visitors need a reason.** A great work, an attraction, or an
  event is what brings them.
- **Visitors need a bed.** A day-trip visitor spends less than a
  hotel guest.
- **Capacity is regional.** *Capacity for 900 additional monthly
> visitors, shared across your cities.*
- **Events have a cost.** Book one when the treasury can afford it.
