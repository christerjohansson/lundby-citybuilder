# Commerce and shoppers

> *Commerce and shoppers* / *From factory to front door.*

Commerce is the city's daily economy. Households shop for goods,
businesses sell them, and factories make them. The simulation runs
the whole loop: production, distribution, sale, and replenishment.

## The commerce loop

1. **Production** — factories and processors turn raw materials into
   goods.
2. **Distribution** — trucks and freight move goods to shops.
3. **Sale** — households and visitors buy goods.
4. **Replenishment** — shops reorder when stock runs low.

> *From factory to front door* — the flavor line.

### The shopper's journey
1. The household or visitor decides to shop.
2. The simulation finds an affordable shop with stock.
3. The shopper travels — by walk, transit, or car.
4. The shopper buys the goods.
5. The shop's stock is reduced; the household's pantry is filled.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **Local first.** *Local factories first, then imports.* A local
  factory keeps the money in the city.
- **Stockpile strategically.** A full warehouse is a buffer against
  disasters.
- **Match retail to wealth.** A luxury boutique in a lower-income
  block goes bankrupt.
- **Watch the inventory.** *Shelves are empty* is the warning you'll
  see when a shop is out of stock.
