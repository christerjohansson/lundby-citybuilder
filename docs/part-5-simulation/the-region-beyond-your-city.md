# The region beyond your city

> *Beyond the city: the region and its neighbors.*

When you connect your city to the edge of the map, you'll see the
**regional map** — a schematic of the region, your neighbours, and
the connections between them.

## The regional view

> *The region beyond your city.*

The regional map shows:

- **Your city** in the center (or wherever it is).
- **Neighbouring cities** — *Cedar Hollow*, *Eastbank*, *Silver
  Coast*, and any other founded municipalities.
- **Schematic corridors** — the regional roads, rail, and sea
  connections.

> *Schematic regional corridors* — the map's roads are schematic,
> not to scale.

## The neighbours

The named neighbours you'll see most often:

- **Cedar Hollow** — *Cedar Valley* / *Cedar fields* / *Cedar
> residences.* The inland neighbour.
- **Eastbank** — *Eastbank.* The eastern neighbour.
- **Silver Coast** — *Silver Coast.* The southern neighbour.

Other named places that appear in the simulation:

- *Port Sterling* — a port city.
- *Capital City* — the capital.
- *Lundby City* — your home city.
- *World city* / *Metropolis* / *Suncoast* / *Westbridge* /
> *Juniper* / *Highland City* — other named places.

## Beyond the city

> *Beyond the city: the region and its neighbors — Cedar Hollow,
> Eastbank, and Silver Coast.*

The chapter title sets the tone: your city is one part of a larger
region. The map shows you the rest.

## How to use the regional map

1. Open the **Region & trade** panel from the management menu.
2. Click **View the entire map** to enter the regional view.
3. Click any city to switch to it (or to see its summary).
4. Click **Back to the city** to return.

> *Back to city* / *Back to the city* / *Back to your city* — the
> return actions.

## What the regional map shows

- **Cities** — each founded municipality.
- **Connections** — *Dashed: rail · Dotted: sea · Schematic regional
> corridors.*
- **Trades** — the active trade routes.
- **Service sharing** — the departments that span the region.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **The regional map is not the world map.** It's just the cities in
  your region.
- **Neighbours are not free.** You have to **found** them, even if
  they're already named.
- **Connections are schematic.** The map doesn't reflect the actual
  road distances.
