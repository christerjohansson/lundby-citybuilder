# Regional cities

> *Connected through your region.*

Lundby is not just one city. It's a region. You can found multiple
municipalities in the same region, connect them with regional roads,
share services, and trade between them.

## Founding a new city

1. Open the **Region & trade** panel.
2. Click **Start a new municipality**.
3. Pick a plot.
4. Give the city a name.
5. *A new city charter costs $20,000 from this city's treasury.*
6. The new city is founded; you can switch to it from the regional
   map.

> *Found city ·* — the confirmation.
> *Found your city* — the title for a new founding.
> *New regional city name* — the prompt.
> *Choose another founded city* — the picker.
> *A new city charter costs $20,000 from this city's treasury.* —
> the cost.
> *The receiving warehouses have no unreserved storage space* — the
> related warning.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **A region is one save.** Treat it as a single object.
- **Found cities when you can afford them.** A charter is $20,000.
- **Connect with regional roads.** A ferry is not a regional road.
- **Share with intent.** Shared services are cheaper, but the
  operating city pays the bill.
