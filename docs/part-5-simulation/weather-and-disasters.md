# Weather and disasters

> *When nature has other plans.*

Lundby has a live weather system and a full set of disasters. Weather
changes the lighting and the simulation; disasters test your city.

## Weather

The default weather is **clear skies**. Other states include:

- *CloudCheck* / *CloudFog* / *CloudLightning* — the icons.
- *Light rain* — the common variant.
- *Rainy day fund* — the budget line (named for the rain).
- *Coastal mist* — the coastal variant.
- *Coastal birds — gliding and flapping* — the bird life.
- *Fog: upgrade to level 2 for instrument approaches* — the airport
  warning.
- *Next day forecast* / *Next shift forecast* — the forecast panel.
- *Clear skies* — the default state.
- *Fog* — the dense variant.
- *Fog: upgrade to level 2* — the airport warning.
- *Ground hold: fog requires instrument approaches* — the airport
  ground hold.

## The disaster controls

> *Disaster controls* — the panel.
> *Rare emergencies require a resilient city.* — the warning.
> *Reduce disaster damage and speed up fire response.* — the
> research effect.

You can:

- **Pause disasters** — turn the disaster system off.
- **Trigger a disaster** — fire one off manually for a test.
- **Set the difficulty** — disasters are tuned to the city's
  difficulty.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **Disasters are not random.** You can pause them when you're not
  ready.
- **The simulation must keep up.** Some disasters (floods) advance one
  calendar day at a time.
- **Repair takes time.** A damaged building doesn't fix itself.
- **Disasters are part of the game.** *Rare emergencies require a
> resilient city.*
