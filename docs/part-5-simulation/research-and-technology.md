# Research and technology

> *Lay the foundations for a city that keeps learning.*

Research is how your city advances. The **Horizon Institute** runs
research projects, and the **Universities** supply the workforce. The
result is a citywide effect that improves one of the simulation's
core numbers.

## The Horizon Institute

> *Build a Horizon Institute* / *Build a Horizon Institute* — the
> build prompt.
> *Horizon Institute* — the building.

The Institute is where research happens. You need:

- A built, operating **Horizon Institute** in your city.
- A connected, operating **university** to staff it.
- A **research campus** to host the work.
- Cash to fund the project.

> *Choose a Horizon Institute in this city.*
> *Assign an institute to resume this project.*
> *The source city needs an operating, road-connected university.* —
> the prerequisite for shared projects.

### The major research effects

| Project | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| *Reduce electricity demand by 25 %.* | Cuts city power use. |
| *Reduce water consumption by 30 %.* | Cuts city water use. |
| *Reduce household waste by 35 %.* | Cuts city waste generation. |
| *Reduce disaster damage and speed up fire response.* | Improves safety. |
| *Research reduces citywide power consumption by 20 %.* | A second power project. |
| *Double transit effectiveness and improve happiness.* | Boosts transit. |
| *Citywide education improvement of 15 points.* | Boosts education. |
| *Citywide health improvement of 12 points.* | Boosts health. |

> *Equipment this project unlocks* — the unlock list.
> *Equipment available to purchase* — the same list, elsewhere.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **One project at a time.** A city can run a single research project
  at a time. Plan accordingly.
- **University first.** The Institute can't run without a connected
  university.
- **Research is slow.** A project can take months of game time. Don't
  expect overnight results.
- **Some effects stack.** Power and water reductions add up.
