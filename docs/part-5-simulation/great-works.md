# Great works

> *An extraordinary city, shaped by you.*

Great works are the largest, most distinctive buildings in Lundby. They
take a long time to build, they cost a lot, and they change the
character of the city. They're the projects you remember.

## What is a great work?

A great work is a unique building that:

- Has its own design (not a variant of a standard building).
- Is built through a **development project**, not the regular build
  menu.
- Has its own **annexes and variants**.
- Unlocks a unique effect or event.

> *Build what comes next.* / *Build something wonderful.* — the
> flavor lines for the great works.

## How a great work is built

### Step 1 — Start the project
1. Open the **Great works** panel from the build menu.
2. Pick a great work.
3. *Plan this project.*
4. The project appears in the management panel.

> *Build a Horizon Institute* / *Build a Horizon Institute* — the
> project prompt when the prerequisite is missing.

### Step 2 — Fund the project
Great works are funded with **development bonds** or with a mix of cash
and milestone grants.

> *Not enough funds for this institute project.* — the cash warning.
> *Funded ·* — the funded state.

### Step 3 — Supply the project
Some great works need delivered materials.

> *Great-work construction sites receive supplies by road*
> *Great-work materials* — the materials panel.
> *This great work is no longer accepting materials* — the closure
> warning.
> *The receiving warehouses have no unreserved storage space* — the
> warehouse warning.

### Step 4 — Wait for completion
> *DEVELOPMENT IN PROGRESS* — the project status.
> *DEVELOPMENT WAITING* — the waiting state.
> *Project cleared. Any undelivered supplies are returning.* — the
> completion.
> *This city has already completed the project.* — the cap.

## Operating a great work

Once a great work is complete, it starts operating.

> *Operating · serving the region* — the status.
> *Your regional great work is now serving connected cities.* — the
> shared effect.
> *View regional great works* — the menu link.

### Operating costs
- *Pay per worker / month* — the staff cost.
- *Maintenance / month* — the upkeep.
- *Service funding* — the operating budget.

## Regional great works

A great work can be **shared across the region**.

> *SHARED REGIONAL PROJECT* — the project type.
> *Regional project funding* — the funding source.
> *A regional connection and available funds are required.* — the
> prerequisites.
> *Both municipalities need an open regional road connection.* — the
> cross-municipality rule.
> *An unfunded regional project cannot be under construction.* — the
> cash rule.
> *The regional project is invalid.* / *The regional project ledger
> is invalid.* / *The shared terminal crew or cargo railway records
> are damaged.* / *The regional cargo, delivery or freight-payment
> records are damaged.* — the error states.

## Annexes and crowns

Many great works accept **annexes** or a **roof crown** in the tower
editor.

> *Complete all eight levels before building a crown.*
> *Choose a roof crown.* / *One roof crown* / *Roof crown catalog*
> — the crown UI.
> *Remove the existing crown before replacing it.*
> *Crown installed* — the confirmation.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **Plan the cost.** A great work can take a year of game time and
  most of your treasury.
- **Plan the site.** A great work needs a flat, road-accessible
  location with utilities.
- **Plan the materials.** Some great works need delivered supplies.
- **Plan the operating cost.** A great work that's not earning its
  keep is a drag on the budget.
