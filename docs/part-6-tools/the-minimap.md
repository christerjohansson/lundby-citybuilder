# The minimap

> *City minimap. Click to move the camera.*

The minimap is a small overview of the city in the corner of the
screen. It's a click-to-move map: click anywhere on it and the camera
moves to that location.

## What the minimap shows

- **The whole city** — a top-down view of the map.
- **The active camera location** — a small frame or arrow showing
  where the main camera is.
- **Selected buildings** — a highlight on the building you've
  selected.
- **Data layer overlays** — if a data layer is on, the minimap
  shows the same colour coding.

> *City minimap. Click to move the camera.* — the description.

## How to use it

- **Click** anywhere on the minimap to move the main camera to that
  location.
- **Drag** to pan the main camera (depending on the version).
- **Right-drag** to orbit the main camera.
- **Scroll** to zoom the main camera.

> *View entire map* — the button that zooms the main camera to
> the full city.

## What you'll see on the minimap

- **Roads** — drawn in their road colour.
- **Buildings** — drawn as small shapes.
- **Water** — drawn in blue.
- **Terrain** — drawn with shading.
- **Data layer** — if a layer is on, the colours match.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **The minimap is not the world.** It's a 2D representation of
  your city, not the region.
- **The minimap is the easiest way to navigate.** Click and the
  camera moves.
- **The minimap reflects the data layers.** Toggle a layer and
  the minimap updates.
