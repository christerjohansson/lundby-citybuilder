# The budget reports

> *The budget needs attention.*

The budget reports are where the city's money is tracked over time.
They're the deepest view into the treasury, the income, the expenses,
and the balance.

## Where to find them

- **City → Budget & economy → Budget reports**
- Or open the **CITY PULSE** panel and click **Budget reports**.

## The three views

- *Latest 12* — the last 12 months.
- *Latest 36* — the last 36 months.
- *All time* — every recorded month since the city began.

> *Scrollable budget snapshots* — the navigation.
> *Budget history range* — the selector.
> *Budget history* — the chart.

## The summary

At the top of the report:

- *Money in* / *Money out* — the month's totals.
- *Net movement* — the difference.
- *Closing balance* — the new balance.
- *Opening balance* — the previous balance.
- *Income & cost breakdown* — the per-category view.

> *Recorded operating balance over time* — the chart.
> *Operating balance and treasury snapshots* — the report.
> *Scheduled balance* / *Scheduled monthly plan* — the forecast.
> *Scheduled income* / *Scheduled expenses* — the breakdown.
> *Next cash transactions* / *Previous cash transactions* — the
> upcoming and past.
> *Statement period* / *Cash statement period* — the period labels.
> *Statement totals by reason* — the breakdown by category.

## The line items

The report groups lines by category:

- **Income & cost breakdown** — the high-level summary.
- **Outlook income, including activity** — the projected income.
- **Outlook expenses, including activity** — the projected expenses.
- **MONTHLY OPERATING OUTLOOK** — the section header.
- **Residential & business taxes** — the tax line.
- **Road & rail maintenance** — the infrastructure line.
- **Service funding** — the services line.

> *All spending so far* / *All time* — the cumulative counter.
> *Average savings reserve* — the buffer.
> *Savings before* / *Savings after* — the bond impact.
> *Lifetime ·* — the all-time label.

> *Last settlement* — the last closed month.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **The budget is monthly.** Don't read too much into a single day's
  movement.
- **The cash journal is the source of truth.** If the journal
  doesn't match the treasury, something is wrong.
- **Bonds and grants are not income.** They're separately labelled.
- **Service funding is a budget line.** Turn it up to run more
  service.
