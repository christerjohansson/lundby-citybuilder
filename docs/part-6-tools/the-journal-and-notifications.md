The journal is the live log of everything that's happening in your
city. It's where you go to see what just happened, what needs
attention, and what to do next.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **The journal is searchable.** Use the search field to find past
  events.
- **Notifications are the high-priority subset.** The journal has
  more.
- **The journal is per-city.** Each city has its own journal.
- **Some events are transient.** A "Fire at" event will scroll off
  the top of the list eventually.
