# The employment views

> *Jobs & workplaces*

The employment views are the live reports on the city's workforce —
every worker, every job, every job move, every commute. This is
where you go when a workplace can't find staff, a household can't
find a job, or a commute is too long.

## Where to find them

- **City → Employment → Employment views**

> *Employment views* — the section.
> *Jobs & workplaces* / *Workforce skills* — the reports.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **The employment view is live.** A worker can change jobs while
  you're looking at the report.
- **Commute failures are common.** A broken road or a closed
  station will leave a worker without a job.
- **Population follows happiness.** A clean, well-served city
  attracts workers.
- **Education is slow.** A university takes years to train its
  first specialist.
