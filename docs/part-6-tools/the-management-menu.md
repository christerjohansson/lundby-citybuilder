# The management menu

> *Open city menu.*

The management menu is the cockpit of your city. Every report, every
panel, every setting lives here. Open it with the menu button or by
pressing the assigned shortcut.

## The sections

The management menu is divided into a few top-level sections. Each
section has its own set of panels.

- **CITY CONTROL NETWORK** — the control capacity.

## The journal

> *Journal* / *The city journal* / *THE DAILY CONNECTION* — the
> journal labels.
> *City notifications* / *City journal* / *RECENT ACTIVITY* — the
> same.
> *Municipal cash journal* — the cash journal.
> *The city’s cash journal.* — the flavor line.
> *The cash journal does not match the treasury.* — the warning.

The journal is the live log of everything that's happening in your
city. It records:

- **INCIDENT IN PROGRESS** events.
- *Active calls* / *Active incidents* — the live counters.
- *Milestone grant* — the credit events.
- *Franchise active* — the corporate events.
- *Building repaired* / *Building upgrade* — the maintenance
> events.
- *Construction in progress* — the build events.
- *City commit* — the simulation commits.

## Tips & common pitfalls

- **The management menu is a lot.** Use the search bar to find what
  you need.
- **Most panels have filters.** A filter that hides everything is
  often the cause of a missing report.
- **The journal is searchable.** Use the search field to find past
  events.
- **The data layers are in the management menu, not the build menu.**
  See [The data layers](the-data-layers.md).
